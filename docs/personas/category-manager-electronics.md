# Persona: Category Manager - Electronics & Technology

## Basic Information
- **Name**: Marcus Rodriguez
- **Age**: 35
- **Location**: MegaMart Category Office, Austin, TX
- **Department**: Electronics Merchandising & Sourcing
- **Reporting Structure**: Reports to VP of Electronics, manages 12 sourcing specialists
- **Experience**: 12 years in retail sourcing, 5 years at MegaMart

## Role & Responsibilities
### Primary Responsibilities
- Managing $8B annual electronics sourcing across consumer electronics, computers, and mobile devices
- Supplier relationship management for 500+ electronics suppliers globally
- Product assortment planning and pricing strategy for electronics categories
- Quality assurance and compliance management
- New product introduction and innovation pipeline management

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Category profitability: 18% gross margin target
- Supplier diversification: Maximum 30% spend with single supplier
- Product availability: 95%+ in-stock rate
- Quality metrics: <2% return rate due to defects
- Time to market: 90-day average for new product introduction

## Pain Points & Challenges
### Current State Problems
- **High Tariff Exposure**: Electronics category most impacted by China tariffs (70% of suppliers)
- **Supplier Concentration Risk**: Over-reliance on handful of large suppliers
- **Complex Supply Chains**: Multi-tier supplier relationships difficult to track
- **Quality vs. Cost Trade-offs**: Pressure to maintain margins while ensuring quality
- **Rapid Technology Changes**: Short product lifecycles requiring agile sourcing

### Tariff-Specific Challenges
- Daily price volatility due to tariff uncertainty
- Difficulty identifying cost-competitive alternatives outside China
- Quality concerns with new suppliers in alternative regions
- Longer lead times when switching to new suppliers
- Customer price sensitivity limiting pass-through options

## Goals & Objectives
### Strategic Goals
- Reduce China dependency from 70% to 40% within 18 months
- Maintain category profitability despite tariff pressures
- Build supplier ecosystem in Vietnam, India, and Mexico
- Accelerate private label development to control costs

### Specific System Goals
- Quickly identify qualified electronics suppliers by region and capability
- Automate supplier risk scoring with technology-specific factors
- Track competitor sourcing strategies and supplier moves
- Streamline qualification process for new suppliers

- **Proficiency**: Power user of business applications

## User Journey & Workflow
### Daily Activities
1. **Market Monitoring** (1 hour): Check tariff updates, competitor pricing, supplier news
2. **Supplier Communications** (2 hours): Calls with existing and potential suppliers
3. **Category Analysis** (2 hours): Sales performance, margin analysis, assortment gaps
4. **Cross-functional Meetings** (2 hours): Merchandising, marketing, operations alignment
5. **Supplier Evaluations** (1 hour): Review new supplier proposals and qualifications

### Weekly Activities
- Supplier performance scorecards and QBRs
- Category strategy updates and planning
- New product development reviews
- Competitive intelligence analysis
- Risk assessment and mitigation planning

### Key Decision Points
- Supplier selection for new products or categories
- Volume allocation decisions among suppliers
- Quality issue escalation and resolution
- Pricing and margin optimization decisions
- Emergency supplier switching during disruptions

## Use Case Scenarios
### Scenario 1: Urgent Supplier Diversification
**Situation**: Major supplier announces 20% price increase due to new tariffs
**Current Process**: 2-3 weeks to manually research alternatives
**Desired Process**: Within 24 hours, system provides ranked list of alternative suppliers with capability match, risk scores, and pricing estimates

### Scenario 2: New Product Sourcing
**Situation**: Need to source innovative smart home device for private label
**Current Process**: Rely on trade shows and existing relationships
**Desired Process**: System suggests suppliers based on technology capabilities, innovation track record, and risk profile

### Scenario 3: Quality Crisis Management
**Situation**: Quality issues discovered with key supplier
**Current Process**: Scramble to find alternatives while managing customer impact
**Desired Process**: Pre-qualified backup suppliers already identified with risk assessments current
