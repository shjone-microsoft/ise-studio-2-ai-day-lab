# Persona: Supplier Risk Analyst

## Basic Information
- **Name**: Priya Patel
- **Age**: 29
- **Location**: MegaMart Supplier Intelligence Center, Plano, TX
- **Department**: Supply Chain Risk Management
- **Reporting Structure**: Reports to Director of Risk Management, individual contributor
- **Experience**: 6 years in supply chain analysis, 3 years at MegaMart

## Role & Responsibilities
### Primary Responsibilities
- Continuous monitoring and assessment of supplier financial health and operational risk
- Developing and maintaining supplier risk scoring models and methodologies
- Conducting deep-dive risk assessments for critical suppliers
- Creating risk reports and recommendations for procurement teams
- Managing supplier risk database and ensuring data quality

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Risk prediction accuracy: >85% for high-risk suppliers
- Assessment completion time: Average 3 days for standard suppliers
- Data quality metrics: >95% accuracy in supplier information
- Early warning effectiveness: Identify 80% of supplier issues before impact
- Report turnaround: Same-day alerts for critical risk changes

## Pain Points & Challenges
### Current State Problems
- **Manual Data Collection**: Spending 70% of time gathering data from disparate sources
- **Lagging Indicators**: Most risk signals are historical, not predictive
- **Information Overload**: Difficult to prioritize risks across thousands of suppliers
- **Limited Real-time Visibility**: Risk assessments become outdated quickly
- **Communication Gaps**: Risk insights not effectively reaching decision-makers

### Tariff-Specific Challenges
- Lack of real-time tariff impact modeling tools
- Difficulty quantifying cascading effects of trade policy changes
- No systematic way to track supplier geographic diversification
- Challenge in correlating supplier financial stress with tariff exposure
- Limited visibility into supplier's own supply chain risks

## Goals & Objectives
### Strategic Goals
- Transform risk management from reactive to predictive
- Build comprehensive supplier risk intelligence capabilities

### Specific System Goals
