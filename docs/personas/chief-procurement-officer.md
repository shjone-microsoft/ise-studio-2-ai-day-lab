# Persona: Chief Procurement Officer (CPO)

## Basic Information
- **Name**: Sarah Chen
- **Age**: 45
- **Location**: MegaMart Corporate Headquarters, Bentonville, AR
- **Department**: Global Procurement
- **Reporting Structure**: Reports to CEO, manages 150+ procurement professionals globally
- **Experience**: 20+ years in procurement, 8 years at MegaMart

## Role & Responsibilities
### Primary Responsibilities
- Strategic sourcing and supplier relationship management across $45B annual spend
- Managing supplier risk and ensuring supply chain resilience
- Leading cost reduction initiatives and negotiating enterprise-wide contracts
- Overseeing supplier diversity programs and sustainability initiatives
- Crisis management during supply chain disruptions

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Annual cost savings targets: $2B+
- Supplier risk score improvements: <2.5 average risk rating
- Supply chain disruption minimization: <48 hours to alternative sourcing
- Supplier diversity spend: 15% target
- Contract compliance rate: >98%

## Pain Points & Challenges
### Current State Problems
- **Reactive Crisis Management**: Spending 60% of time firefighting tariff-related supplier issues
- **Limited Supplier Visibility**: Difficult to assess true risk exposure across 15,000+ suppliers
- **Manual Risk Assessment**: Time-consuming quarterly supplier reviews with outdated data
- **Siloed Information**: Risk data scattered across multiple systems and spreadsheets
- **Slow Decision Making**: Takes weeks to identify and qualify alternative suppliers

### Tariff-Specific Challenges
- Lack of real-time tariff impact visibility across supplier portfolio
- Difficulty prioritizing which supplier relationships need immediate attention
- No automated way to identify geographically diverse alternatives
- Challenges in communicating risk levels to C-suite and board

## Goals & Objectives
### Strategic Goals
- Transform procurement from reactive to predictive
- Build a resilient supplier network that can withstand trade disruptions
- Achieve cost savings while maintaining quality and service levels
- Establish MegaMart as the "customer of choice" for suppliers

### Specific System Goals
- Reduce supplier risk assessment time by 80%
- Identify alternative suppliers within 24 hours of risk alerts
- Improve supplier diversification across all critical categories
- Automate supplier scoring and qualification processes

## Technology Profile
### Current Tools & Systems
- SAP Ariba for procurement processes
- Various supplier portals and databases
- Excel spreadsheets for risk tracking
- Email and phone for supplier communication
- PowerBI for reporting and dashboards

### Technology Comfort Level
- **Proficiency**: Advanced user of enterprise software
- **Adoption Style**: Quick adopter of solutions that demonstrate clear ROI
- **Preferences**: Clean, intuitive interfaces with powerful analytics
- **Frustrations**: Complex systems that require extensive training

## User Journey & Workflow
### Daily Activities
1. **Morning Risk Review** (30 mins): Check overnight alerts and risk score changes
2. **Supplier Meetings** (2-3 hours): Video calls with key suppliers and internal stakeholders
3. **Strategic Planning** (2 hours): Long-term supplier strategy and relationship planning
4. **Crisis Response** (Variable): Address urgent supplier issues and disruptions
5. **Reporting** (1 hour): Update executives on procurement metrics and risks

### Weekly Activities
- Supplier performance reviews and scoreboard updates
- Cross-functional meetings with operations, finance, and legal teams
- Vendor relationship strategy sessions
- Board and executive reporting preparation

### Key Decision Points
- Supplier selection and qualification approvals
- Risk mitigation strategy approvals
- Emergency supplier switching decisions
- Budget allocation for supplier development

## Behavioral Characteristics
### Communication Style
- **Internal**: Direct, data-driven, focused on business impact
- **External**: Diplomatic, relationship-focused, collaborative
- **Reporting**: Executive-level summaries with detailed backup data available

### Decision-Making Style
- Analytical and methodical for strategic decisions
- Quick and decisive during crisis situations
- Values peer input but takes ownership of final decisions
- Risk-aware but not risk-averse

### Motivations & Values
- Protecting MegaMart's operational continuity
- Building long-term supplier partnerships
- Driving measurable business value
- Professional reputation and career advancement

### Must-Have Features
- Real-time supplier risk scoring with tariff impact analysis
- Automated alternative supplier identification and ranking
- Integration with existing procurement systems (SAP Ariba)

### Success Metrics
