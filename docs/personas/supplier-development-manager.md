# Persona: Supplier Development Manager

## Basic Information
- **Name**: David Thompson
- **Age**: 38
- **Location**: MegaMart Asia Pacific Office, Hong Kong
- **Department**: Supplier Development & Qualification
- **Reporting Structure**: Reports to Regional Sourcing Director, manages 6 development specialists
- **Experience**: 14 years in supplier development, 4 years at MegaMart

## Role & Responsibilities
### Primary Responsibilities
- Leading new supplier qualification and capability development programs
- Managing supplier onboarding processes and compliance certification
- Conducting supplier audits and capability assessments
- Developing supplier improvement roadmaps and monitoring progress
- Coordinating with local teams across Asia Pacific region

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Supplier qualification timeline: Average 90 days for standard suppliers
- New supplier success rate: >85% successful integration within 12 months
- Supplier capability improvement: 20% annual improvement in key metrics
- Audit compliance: 100% completion of scheduled audits
- Regional diversification: 40% reduction in single-country dependency

## Pain Points & Challenges
### Current State Problems
- **Resource Constraints**: High demand for new suppliers, limited assessment capacity
- **Standardization Issues**: Inconsistent qualification criteria across categories
- **Language and Cultural Barriers**: Communication challenges with diverse suppliers
- **Quality Variability**: Wide range in supplier maturity and capabilities
- **Documentation Overhead**: Extensive paperwork and compliance requirements

### Tariff-Specific Challenges
- Pressure to accelerate qualification timelines without compromising standards
- Need to assess suppliers in new, unfamiliar markets (Vietnam, India, Mexico)
- Balancing speed with thorough due diligence on new suppliers
- Managing increased travel and assessment costs across multiple countries
- Coordinating with headquarters on rapidly changing requirements
