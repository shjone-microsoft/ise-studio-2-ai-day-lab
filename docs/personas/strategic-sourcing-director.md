# Persona: Strategic Sourcing Director

## Basic Information
- **Name**: Jennifer Kim
- **Age**: 42
- **Location**: MegaMart Regional Office, Chicago, IL (covers North America sourcing)
- **Department**: Strategic Sourcing - Global Operations
- **Reporting Structure**: Reports to CPO, manages 8 category teams (45 people total)
- **Experience**: 18 years in procurement and sourcing, 6 years at MegaMart

## Role & Responsibilities
### Primary Responsibilities
- Strategic supplier portfolio management across multiple categories ($12B annual spend)
- Leading supplier diversification initiatives and market entry strategies
- Managing complex contract negotiations and supplier relationship governance
- Driving innovation partnerships and sustainability initiatives
- Regional sourcing strategy development and execution

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Supplier diversification metrics: <40% concentration in any single country
- Cost savings delivery: $800M annual target across managed categories
- Supplier innovation pipeline: 20+ new products/innovations annually
- Relationship health scores: >4.0/5.0 average across strategic suppliers
- Contract compliance and risk mitigation: 100% coverage for critical suppliers

## Pain Points & Challenges
### Current State Problems
- **Strategic vs. Tactical Balance**: Too much time on firefighting, not enough on strategy
- **Limited Market Intelligence**: Difficulty identifying emerging suppliers and markets
- **Complex Stakeholder Management**: Balancing competing demands across categories
- **Long Supplier Development Cycles**: 12-18 months to fully qualify new suppliers
- **Measurement Challenges**: ROI of relationship investments hard to quantify

### Tariff-Specific Challenges
- Pressure to rapidly diversify away from concentrated regions
- Balancing speed of diversification with quality and compliance standards
- Managing team capacity during accelerated supplier development
- Communicating progress and trade-offs to executive leadership
- Coordinating across categories for maximum portfolio impact
