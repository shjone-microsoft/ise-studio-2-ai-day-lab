# MegaMart IT Strategic Hypotheses for Tariff Crisis Resolution

## Executive Summary

As CTO of MegaMart, I've developed 10 IT-enabled hypotheses to address our supply chain crisis caused by tariff uncertainty. These solutions leverage technology to build resilience, reduce costs, and maintain competitive advantage during this challenging period.

## Ranked Hypotheses (by Predicted ROI)

### 1. AI-Powered Tariff Prediction & Supply Chain Optimization Platform (ROI Rank: 1)

**Hypothesis**: Implementing an AI/ML system that ingests real-time trade policy data, news sentiment, and economic indicators to predict tariff changes 30-60 days in advance will reduce crisis management costs by 40% and improve inventory optimization.

**Technical Implementation**:
- Natural Language Processing for policy document analysis
- Machine learning models trained on historical tariff patterns
- Integration with government trade databases and news APIs
- Automated supplier switching recommendations

**Expected ROI**: 500-800%
- Cost savings: $50-80M annually from reduced emergency sourcing
- Revenue protection: $100-150M from better inventory availability
- Investment required: $15-25M over 18 months

**Risk Mitigation**: Transforms reactive crisis management into proactive strategic planning
